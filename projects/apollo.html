Program Context

MITES (Minority Introduction to Engineering and Science) is a rigorous six-week residential STEM program hosted at MIT. Accepted students take college-level courses, work on intensive projects, and engage with researchers and engineers across disciplines. I was selected to participate in the summer of 2023.

As part of the program, I enrolled in a 3D animation and visual storytelling course where the capstone deliverable was a fully original animated short — modeled, rigged, lit, and rendered from scratch with no pre-built assets.

Technical Challenges & Solutions

Modeling & Scale
  • Zero Prior Experience: Learned polygon modeling, UV unwrapping, material nodes, keyframe animation, and camera rigging within the first two weeks
  • Scale & Proportion: Accurately representing the Saturn V (363 ft tall) relative to the launchpad required careful reference study and scene unit management
VFX & Environments
  • Rocket Engine Exhaust: Simulated engine plume effects using Maya's particle and fluid systems — the most technically complex element of the project
  • Lunar Surface: Built a procedural lunar environment using displacement maps and custom shaders to achieve the correct low-albedo, cratered appearance without texture libraries
Rendering & Performance
  • Render Optimization: Managed render times on shared lab hardware by optimizing polygon counts, using instancing for repeated geometry, and baking lighting where possible
  • Hardware Constraints: Delivered a full multi-sequence render on lab computers within the program's timeline
Storytelling & Accuracy
  • Historical Reference: Cross-referenced NASA footage and mission documentation to ensure accurate sequence timing across all five mission phases
  • Camera Work: Developed multiple camera rigs to capture the scale contrast between the Saturn V and the lunar surface

Outcome & Reflection

  • Delivered on time: Completed a polished, fully rendered animated short within the 6-week program timeline
  • Skill acquisition: Gained Maya proficiency from zero — a skill that now informs how I think about 3D geometry in mechanical CAD
  • Presented publicly: Screened the final animation at the MITES end-of-program showcase for faculty, staff, and peers
  • Cross-disciplinary insight: Deepened appreciation for the intersection of engineering accuracy and visual communication
