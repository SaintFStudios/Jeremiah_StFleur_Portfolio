Hand-Powered French Fry Slicer — EML2023 Final Project

A reverse-guillotine fry slicer — dovetail-guided pusher plate, spring return, and interchangeable stainless blade grids, fully modeled in SolidWorks.

Fall 2023 CAD & Mechanical Design
CAD SolidWorks Mechanism Design GD&T Design for Assembly

Project Overview

Final project for EML2023 (Computer Aided Graphics and Design) at the University of Florida, taught by Dr. Ashok V. Kumar — the course where I was formally taught SolidWorks and the fundamentals of solid modeling, assembly design, and GD&T, though I was already fairly comfortable with CAD going in. The assignment was open-ended: independently design any slicing mechanism and fully model it. I designed a hand-powered potato slicer built to cut French fries quickly in a home kitchen, submitted November 28, 2023.

Engineering Documentation

From guillotine to reverse guillotine — mechanism reasoning, return system, materials, and design-for-assembly decisions.

Mechanism
  • First idea: a guillotine blade slamming down — abandoned before sketching, no clean way to deposit slices
  • Reverse guillotine: pusher plate pushes the potato down onto stationary blades
  • Guide slots: switched from square to dovetail profile for better guidance
  • 35° cutting angle: gravity holds the potato in place and keeps it from misaligning
Return System
  • Return springs housed in the same dovetail slots the pusher plate rides in
  • Plate snaps back to the top on its own after each cut
  • Pushing is easier than pulling — the spring does the harder job
  • Injection-molded spring covers press-fit into slots, adhesive at the base against loosening
Materials
  • Main body: ABS + PC coating, 3D-printed — complex angled geometry ruled out injection molding
  • Blades & springs: 304 stainless steel — food-safe, corrosion and heat resistant
  • First project requiring material choices justified against food safety, cost, and manufacturability
Design for Assembly
  • Blade grid and basket slide into dedicated slots with free-running clearance — no tools
  • Both removable and dishwasher-safe for cleaning
  • Blade handle doubles as a wall-storage hole; basket handle has a semi-circle undercut for pulling
